Westral Fashion was created with a simple vision — to provide premium quality fashion that combines elegance, comfort, and affordability. We believe fashion is more than clothing; it is a way to express confidence and individuality.

Our platform offers a carefully curated collection of clothing, shoes, accessories, and seasonal trends for men and women. Every product is selected with attention to quality, style, and customer satisfaction.

Based in Kozhikode, Kerala, we ship across India and are committed to making premium fashion accessible to everyone.

Privacy Policy

At Westral Fashion, we are committed to protecting your personal information. We collect only the data necessary to provide you with the best shopping experience.

What we collect: Name, email address, shipping address, payment information, and browsing behavior on our platform.

How we use it: To process orders, send order updates, provide customer support, and improve our services. We never sell your data to third parties.

Security: All data is encrypted and stored securely. Payments are processed through Razorpay's secure gateway.

Terms & Conditions

By using Westral Fashion, you agree to the following terms:

  • You must be 18 years or older to place an order.
  • All product images are for illustrative purposes; slight color variations may exist.
  • Westral Fashion reserves the right to cancel any order due to stock unavailability.
  • Referral rewards are subject to terms and may be revised at any time.
  • Misuse of the platform will result in account suspension.

Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

  • Return requests must be raised within 7 days of delivery.
  • Products must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging.
  • Refunds are processed to your Westral Wallet within 3–5 business days after return inspection.
  • Wallet balance can be used for future purchases on our platform.
  • COD orders: refund is issued as wallet credit.

Shipping Policy

We ship to all major pin codes across India.

  • Standard delivery: 3–7 business days.
  • Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Free shipping on orders above a certain threshold (check current promotions).
  • Tracking details are shared via email once your order is shipped.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by courier partners or natural events.

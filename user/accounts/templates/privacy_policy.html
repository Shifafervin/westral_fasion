Privacy Policy

How we protect, collect, and handle your data at Westral Fashion.

Introduction

At Westral Fashion, protecting your privacy and security is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit or make a purchase from our site.

What Information We Collect

When you register or shop on our website, we collect personal information that you provide to us, including:

  • Account Details: Your name, username, email address, password, and contact details.
  • Delivery Details: Your shipping and billing addresses to fulfill your orders.
  • Device Details: Information about your browser, IP address, and cookie identifiers to enhance your user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information in the following ways:

  • To process your orders, payments, and verify deliveries.
  • To communicate order status, customer support questions, or promotional deals.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions and keep our platform secure.
  • To analyze and optimize site speed, search capabilities, and product offerings.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Payment processing is handled by Razorpay through secure, industry-standard gateways, and we do not store your credit card or financial credentials on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account. You can manage these details in your Profile section or contact our support team at westralfashion@gmail.com.

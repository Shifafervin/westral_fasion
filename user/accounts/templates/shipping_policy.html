Shipping Policy

Details about delivery coverage, processing times, and shipping fees.

Coverage

Westral Fashion delivers packages across all major pin codes and regions in India. We partner with reliable shipping companies to ensure your fashion arrives safely and quickly.

Processing & Dispatch

Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sunday or national holidays will be shipped on the next business day.

Delivery Timelines

  • Standard Shipping: Typically 3–7 business days depending on your delivery location.
  • Metro cities usually receive packages within 3-4 days, while remote locations may take up to 7 business days.

Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on delivery location and order weight. We offer free shipping on orders above a specified threshold, which is highlighted during promotions on our homepage.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, we will send you a tracking link via email. You can also view details and track your delivery in your My Orders page.

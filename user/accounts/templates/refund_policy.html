Refund & Return Policy

Everything you need to know about returning items and getting refunds.

Return Window

We want you to love your purchases. If you are not completely satisfied, you may request a return within 7 days of delivery. Return requests raised after 7 days will not be accepted.

Conditions for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following criteria:

  • The product must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition that you received it.
  • It must be in its original packaging with all brand tags and labels intact.
  • Items purchased during clearance sales or special bundle promotions may not be eligible for returns (will be specified on product detail page).

How Refund is Processed

Once your returned product is collected by our courier partner and passes inspection at our warehouse (typically 3-5 business days), your refund will be processed:

  • Wallet Refund: The refund amount will be credited directly to your Westral Wallet balance.
  • Wallet Use: Wallet credits can be used instantly to buy any products across the store.
  • COD Orders: Refunds for Cash on Delivery orders will also be processed exclusively as Wallet credits.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or issues with your returns, please reach out to our team at westralfashion@gmail.com.
